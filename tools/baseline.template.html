Event technical details

Crossing paths in the top view do not necessarily imply a collision: aircraft may be at different altitudes or pass the same point at different times. Violations come from precomputed continuous intervals under the selected rules. Equality at a threshold is safe; different distance measures may reach their minima at different times.

All objects retain their initial velocities, including outside the area. The ground target is stationary. The threat passes its horizontal coordinates at 13:53.33 and continues at its original altitude. Altitudes and distances are in km; speeds are in m/s. Boundary counts describe positions, not safety violations.
