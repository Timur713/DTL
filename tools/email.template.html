How safety and optimality were checked

Continuous verification: all 780 aircraft pairs, both spheres, altitude and motion limits, including segment joins. The independent Python checker reads the saved controls and uses rational polynomial sign checks. Result: 0 violations, 0 unresolved checks. Animation samples are for display.

* Endpoint proof: two disjoint terminal pairs need at least 300 m relative vertical displacement each. Triangle inequality gives at least 600 m total and 150 m worst individual deviation. The submitted plan attains both under fixed horizontal motion. Other maneuver costs are search results, not global optima.

Rules, assumptions and the limit of the guarantee

Separation: 9260 m horizontally OR 300 m vertically. Drone and target are 5000 m spheres. Aircraft stay at 3000–12000 m; vertical speed ≤15 m/s and vertical acceleration ≤0.5 m/s². Equality is allowed. These are explicit research assumptions and reference scales.

Exact states and execution, point aircraft, fixed horizontal velocities, no wind or delays. The drone continues past the target. Aircraft remain simulated outside the XY frame. No airport arrival, post-horizon or real-world certification is claimed.

Sensitivity: opposing 1 m altitude errors can reduce binding endpoint separation to 298 m. The alternative 304.8 m threshold produces 16 events. Nominal safety therefore does not establish robust execution safety.
